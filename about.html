About Liv

The story behind the crafts — a 10-year-old with big dreams and a love for making things!

Hi, I'm Liv! 👋

Welcome to Made by Liv! I'm Liv, I'm 10 years old and I absolutely love crafting! Making things has always been my favourite thing to do — I started out doing lots of drawings and that's where I discovered my real talent for creating all sorts of things.

Now I make all kinds of crafts — from handmade jewellery and beautifully decorated glass jars to fun personalised cartoon drawings of people and families! I also love making cool 3D printed items. Every piece I make is special and created with loads of love.

When I'm not busy crafting, you'll probably find me dancing 💃 — it's my other big passion! My dream is to become a professional dancer one day. I also love spending time with my family — they're my biggest supporters and always encourage me to keep creating.

I set up Made by Liv with my dad so I could share my creations with everyone. I hope my crafts bring a smile to your face — every single piece is made with care, creativity, and a whole lot of fun!
